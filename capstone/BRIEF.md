# Capstone project brief

A group project that closes the course: each group picks one of the five case studies in [`../cases/`](../cases/),
analyses it with the data set of its group in [`../data/`](../data/) and presents a recommendation.

## Why a capstone

- **Apply what was taught:** the case brings the course topics (ERP, WMS, TMS, 3PL/4PL, demand planning, data-driven
  decisions) together in one decision.
- **Work like in a company:** groups are assigned, not chosen, so students practise working with colleagues they did
  not pick.
- **Something to show:** the presentation is a piece for the students' portfolio.

## Process (about ten weeks)

| Week | Step |
|---|---|
| 1 | Groups are formed and receive their data set |
| 2–4 | Each group chooses one of the five cases and names a group lead |
| 5–8 | Analysis: requirements, options, data analysis, recommendation |
| 9–10 | Slides finished and rehearsed |
| last week | 20-minute presentation per group, then questions; slides are handed in the same day |

## Assessment criteria

- **Depth of knowledge:** how thoroughly the group explored the case and the options.
- **Creativity and originality:** is the proposed solution well argued and new?
- **Quality of execution:** analysis, use of the data, structure and delivery of the presentation.
- **Relevance and impact:** can fellow students follow and use the result?

Weighting used in the course: 30 % for the submitted slides, 40 % for the presentation in class (the remaining 30 %
came from other course work).

## Using the data

Every group has the same four tables with its own numbers (see [`../data/README.md`](../data/README.md)). Typical
tasks: forecast demand per SKU, compare inventory with reorder point and safety stock, find the supplier with the
shortest and most reliable lead time, and show regional sales and returns. Each case asks a system or partner
decision; the data make the argument concrete.

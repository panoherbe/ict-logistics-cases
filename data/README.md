# Practice data (synthetic)

Ten groups, four tables each, all numbers **synthetic** (generated in November 2024 for a group exercise on big data
and data-driven decisions, no real company data). Every group gets the same structure with its own values, so groups
cannot copy results from each other.

| File | Columns | Rows | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| `demand_data.csv` | Date, SKU, Demand | 60 | monthly demand of 5 SKUs, January to December 2024 |
| `inventory_data.csv` | Date, SKU, Inventory Level, Reorder Point, Safety Stock | 60 | monthly stock per SKU |
| `sales_data.csv` | Date, SKU, Region, Sales, Returns | 240 | monthly sales and returns per SKU in four regions |
| `order_lead_times.csv` | SKU, Supplier, Lead Time | 15 | average lead time per supplier (whole days assumed), three suppliers per SKU |

# Case Study 1: Retail Groceries Chain – ERP vs. WMS for Inventory Management

**Company Profile:**
- Name: FreshMart GmbH
- Size: 1,500 employees
- Industry: Retail Groceries
- Headquarters: Berlin, Germany

**Background:**
FreshMart GmbH operates a chain of supermarkets across Germany, specializing in organic and locally sourced products. Recently, FreshMart has faced challenges in maintaining real-time inventory visibility across multiple stores and warehouses. Outdated processes often lead to stockouts of high-demand products, which impacts customer satisfaction and loyalty. Additionally, inventory management is highly manual, and each store operates semi-independently, creating inconsistencies in stock levels.

**Objective:**
FreshMart needs a system that integrates inventory data across all locations and ensures timely replenishment. The company is debating between implementing an ERP system with integrated inventory management or a Warehouse Management System (WMS) that focuses more directly on optimizing storage and logistics within its warehouse facilities.

**Key Challenges:**
- Inventory Visibility and Synchronization: FreshMart needs to ensure that stock levels across all stores and warehouses are accurate and synchronized in real-time.
- Automated Replenishment: The company wants to reduce manual efforts in replenishment and improve forecasting capabilities.
- Scalability and Integration: FreshMart aims to integrate with existing POS systems at store levels to have a unified view but is concerned about ERP complexity and scalability.

**Decision Point:**
Should FreshMart implement an ERP system for a holistic view and integration or adopt a specialized WMS to optimize inventory management specifically within warehouse operations?

**Questions for Discussion:**
- What are the advantages of ERP vs. WMS for FreshMart’s needs?
- How would real-time inventory updates impact FreshMart’s supply chain?
- How could integrating an ERP or WMS with their POS system help FreshMart better manage demand forecasting and stockouts?

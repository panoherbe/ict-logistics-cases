# Case Study 4: Pharmaceutical Distribution – 4PL Model and Demand Planning System for Supply Chain Optimization

**Company Profile:**
- Name: MediServe GmbH
- Size: 1,500 employees
- Industry: Pharmaceutical Distribution
- Headquarters: Frankfurt, Germany

**Background:**
MediServe GmbH is a pharmaceutical distributor serving hospitals, clinics, and pharmacies throughout Germany. The company is under pressure to ensure timely deliveries while managing the strict regulatory requirements of pharmaceuticals. Recent demand fluctuations due to seasonal and emergency needs have led to shortages in high-demand products and overstocking of slower-moving items. Currently, MediServe uses basic forecasting tools, but they lack the sophistication needed to address demand variability.

**Objective:**
MediServe is evaluating whether to partner with a 4PL provider that can manage all logistics and supply chain elements, or to implement a supply-demand planning system that enhances their ability to forecast demand accurately and manage inventory levels.

**Key Challenges:**
- Demand Variability: Pharmaceuticals have volatile demand, especially in emergencies or seasonal spikes.
- Regulatory Compliance: MediServe must comply with strict regulations, including accurate tracking of expiry dates and storage conditions.
- Efficiency and Costs: Managing warehousing and distribution efficiently while controlling costs is essential for profitability.

**Decision Point:**
Should MediServe partner with a 4PL provider to handle logistics and supply chain needs comprehensively, or implement a dedicated supply-demand planning system for precise forecasting and inventory management?

**Questions for Discussion:**
- What advantages does a 4PL provider offer MediServe in managing its logistics and supply chain?
- How could a supply-demand planning system specifically benefit MediServe’s ability to forecast and manage inventory?
- What are the risks and benefits of relying on an external 4PL provider for critical logistics versus building internal planning capabilities?

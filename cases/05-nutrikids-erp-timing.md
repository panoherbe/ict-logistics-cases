# Case Study 5: ERP Implementation Timing for a High-Growth Milk Powder Company

**Company Profile:**
- Name: NutriKids GmbH
- Size: 100 employees (targeting 200 within a year)
- Industry: Infant Nutrition (Milk Powder)
- Headquarters: Munich, Germany

**Background:**
NutriKids GmbH produces high-quality milk powder for infants and toddlers. With recent VC funding, the company plans to expand operations significantly, doubling its workforce and revenue within a year. NutriKids currently sells its products in 10 countries, with plans to increase distribution and build new warehouses in China and Boston to support overseas sales.

To scale quickly, NutriKids needs a strong operational foundation, but its current systems are decentralized and lack the sophistication needed to support rapid growth across global markets. Implementing an Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system could centralize their operations, integrating production, logistics, sales, and customer service. However, with the company’s expansion plans just beginning, there are concerns about the timing of ERP implementation. Some leaders feel that the company should focus first on warehouse and distribution capabilities before taking on ERP integration.

**Objective:**
NutriKids must decide whether to implement an ERP system immediately, which could streamline and centralize their global operations, or to delay ERP integration until they establish more stable operations in their new markets.

**Key Challenges:**
- Operational Scalability: NutriKids needs efficient, integrated systems to manage increased production and distribution while maintaining product quality.
- Global Coordination: With new warehouses in China and Boston, coordinating inventory, shipments, and compliance with local regulations will be critical.
- Resource Allocation: Implementing an ERP requires significant time, budget, and training, which could strain resources during this rapid expansion phase.

**Decision Point:**
Should NutriKids implement an ERP system now to support its rapid growth and global operations, or should it focus first on establishing its international warehousing and distribution capabilities and revisit ERP integration later?

**Questions for Discussion:**
- What are the potential benefits of implementing an ERP system immediately for a high-growth company like NutriKids?
- What risks might NutriKids face by delaying ERP implementation, particularly with international growth?
- How would an ERP system enhance NutriKids' ability to coordinate between German production and overseas warehouses?

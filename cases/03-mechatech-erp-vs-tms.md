# Case Study 3: Machinery Production – ERP vs. TMS for Efficient Logistics and Supply Chain Planning

**Company Profile:**
- Name: MechaTech GmbH
- Size: 1,200 employees
- Industry: Industrial Machinery Manufacturing
- Headquarters: Stuttgart, Germany

**Background:**
MechaTech GmbH designs and manufactures complex machinery for industrial clients across Europe. The company faces challenges in managing logistics for transporting heavy, customized machinery from its production facility to clients. MechaTech currently uses a manual system for logistics and transportation planning, leading to inefficiencies, delayed deliveries, and rising costs. MechaTech wants to streamline its logistics to ensure timely deliveries and improve supply chain visibility.

**Objective:**
The company is considering an ERP system with integrated supply chain modules to centralize operations or a Transportation Management System (TMS) specifically to optimize logistics, plan shipments, and coordinate with carriers.

**Key Challenges:**
- Logistics Complexity: Machinery is heavy, often requires specialized transport, and has strict timelines for client projects.
- Cost Control: Rising transportation and logistics costs are affecting profitability, making cost control critical.
- Visibility and Coordination: MechaTech needs real-time tracking to improve transparency with clients on delivery status.

**Decision Point:**
Should MechaTech implement an ERP for an all-in-one solution or a specialized TMS to focus directly on logistics optimization?

**Questions for Discussion:**
- How would a TMS improve MechaTech’s transportation logistics compared to an ERP?
- What are the trade-offs between ERP’s centralized control and TMS’s logistics-specific capabilities?
- How could improved logistics and visibility enhance customer satisfaction and project efficiency?
